Features

Everything You Need to Manage Your Shop

From ingredient tracking to profit analysis — BrewDesk covers every aspect of your coffee business.

🧂

Ingredient Management

Track stock levels, set low-stock alerts, and manage costs per unit. Never run out of essential ingredients.

☕

Product Recipes

Define recipes with exact ingredient amounts. Automatically calculate product cost and profit margin per item.

💰

Sales Tracking

One-click selling with automatic stock deduction. Real-time daily revenue, cost, and profit calculations.

📊

Profit Analysis

Visual profit margin bars, cost breakdown per product, and leaderboard for your most profitable items.

⚠️

Smart Alerts

Red highlighting for low-stock ingredients. Disable sell buttons when stock is insufficient to fill an order.

🔍

Search & Filter

Instantly search ingredients and filter products. Inline editing and modals for a smooth management experience.

How It Works

Get Started in 3 Simple Steps

1️⃣

Add Ingredients

2️⃣

Build Products

3️⃣

Sell & Profit
